## 🗡️ About the Series
> *"Set your heart ablaze!"*  
Demon Slayer follows the journey of **Tanjiro Kamado**, a kind boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is killed by demons, and his sister **Nezuko** is turned into one.  
The story mixes **intense battles**, **emotional moments**, and **beautiful animation** that makes every episode memorable.

---

## 💖 Why I Love Demon Slayer
- The animation is top-tier (especially the fight scenes 🎬)  
- The soundtrack gives chills  
- Every character has a touching backstory  
- It teaches values like **family, perseverance,** and **compassion**

---

## 🔥 My Favorite Characters
